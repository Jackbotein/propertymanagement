Why it matters

Property management is the hardest job in real estate. When it slips, everyone pays.

Residents

Slow responses and deferred maintenance wear down the homes people depend on.

Housing supply

Avoidable vacancies pull much-needed units off a market that can’t spare them.

Investors

Capital that should be compounding sits in assets that aren’t being run to their potential.

Communities

A neglected building weighs on the block around it — and on the neighbors who live there.

That’s why we believe owners should be working with the best, most trusted firms available — and why we built a more deliberate way to make those connections.

How it works

Data finds the gap. People close it.

AI lets us look across far more properties than any one person could. Experience decides what’s worth a conversation.

  1. 01

    Identify

    Our models screen properties against their markets, flagging assets where performance trails potential and the evidence points to management — not fundamentals.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Before any outreach, we review the signals in context: asset class, market, ownership structure, and what a strong operator could realistically change.

  3. 03

    Talk

    Jack Botein reaches out to ownership directly for a candid, peer-level conversation. No scripts, no pressure — just a clear-eyed look at the asset.

  4. 04

    Introduce

    When there’s a fit, we make a warm introduction to a firm with a proven record on comparable assets — the start of a long-term management partnership.
